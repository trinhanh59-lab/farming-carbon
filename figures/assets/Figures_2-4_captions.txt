Figure 2. Monoculture (light) and polyculture (dark) annual vegetable fields in Fresno County, California, over 0–100 cm. Panels show (a) recovered root mass density, (b) total carbon (C) concentration, (c) cumulative C stock, and (d) bulk-soil radiocarbon (Δ¹⁴C). Panels (a), (b), and (d) show response-scale estimated marginal means ±1 standard error (SE); panel (c) shows observed means ±1 SE (n = 3 cores per field). The density equivalent of the 0.00017-g addition was subtracted from fitted root means for display. Brackets mark unadjusted within-depth comparisons between fields: * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001. In (c), overall ns denotes a nonsignificant Welch analysis of variance comparing the fields for C stock at 0–100 cm (p ≥ 0.05).

Figure 4. Bare margin (lightest), 10-year hedgerow, and 30-year hedgerow (darkest) at the Hardy property in Winters, California, over 0–75 cm. Panels show (a) recovered root mass density, (b) total carbon (C) concentration, (c) cumulative C stock, and (d) bulk-soil radiocarbon (Δ¹⁴C).
